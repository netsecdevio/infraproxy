# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, constructive, and specific when discussing code or disagreeing. Assume good intent while taking reports of harm seriously.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable in project spaces. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other interactions representing this project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close conversations, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportional to the behavior and may include warnings or temporary or permanent bans.

Report violations using GitHub's report-abuse tools or contact a repository maintainer privately through the contact channels they publish on their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive allegations publicly. Maintainers should protect the reporter's privacy and recuse themselves from reports involving their own conduct; GitHub's reporting channel remains available in those cases.
